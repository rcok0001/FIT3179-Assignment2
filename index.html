Conclusion

Since 2015, most regions have improved access to water, electricity, and the internet, but Central Africa continues to lag behind. Limited infrastructure and investment have slowed progress, leaving many communities disconnected from essential services.

  • Invest in infrastructure to reach Central Africa’s underserved areas
  • Support governance and maintenance alongside new development
  • Foster international cooperation to close the global access gap
